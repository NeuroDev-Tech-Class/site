Application: Careers Day Presentation

Now it’s time to put your presentation skills to use by creating a slideshow for Careers Day. This assignment doesn’t come with strict instructions, but it’s an opportunity for you to demonstrate what you’ve learned. The end result will be a polished presentation that you can actually deliver. Give it your best effort!

Create a slideshow presentation about a career that interests you. Include information such as:

  • What the career is and what the day-to-day work looks like
  • Education or training requirements
  • Salary expectations
  • Why this career appeals to you

Use text boxes, images, transitions, animations, speaker notes, and other features to make your presentation engaging and professional. Reach out for help or feedback if you need to.

When you’re done, share your presentation with the tech coach at hayden@neurodevmentoring.com.
