Audio Production — Audacity

Activity: 60-Second Personal Introduction

Record, edit, and export a clean voice recording from start to finish

Overview

In this activity, you'll apply everything you've learned in Unit 1 to create a short personal introduction recording. You'll write a script, set up your microphone, record, edit, and export — the complete audio production workflow.

What You'll Create

A polished 45- to 75-second audio clip where you introduce yourself. The final product should sound clear, be properly leveled, and be free of mistakes and unnecessary silence.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Step 1: Write Your Script

Before you hit Record, write a short script covering the following:

  • Your name (and how you'd like to be addressed).
  • A little about your interests or hobbies (1–2 sentences).
  • A fun fact about yourself.
  • What you're hoping to learn or build in this course.

Aim for about 100–150 words — roughly 45–75 seconds when spoken naturally. Read it aloud a couple of times to make sure it flows and feels comfortable.

Tip: Write in a conversational tone. You're not giving a formal speech — imagine you're introducing yourself to a new classmate.

Step 2: Set Up Your Microphone

  1. Open Audacity.
  2. Select your microphone as the input device (Audio Setup toolbar or Edit > Preferences > Audio Settings).
  3. Set the channel to 1 (Mono).
  4. Click the Recording Meter to start monitoring.
  5. Speak at your normal volume and adjust the recording level so peaks land between -12 dB and -6 dB.

Step 3: Record Your Introduction

  1. Press R to start recording.
  2. Wait 2–3 seconds of silence, then read your script naturally.
  3. If you make a mistake, pause briefly, then re-read the sentence from the beginning. You'll edit out the mistake later.
  4. Leave another 2–3 seconds of silence at the end.
  5. Press Space to stop recording.
Tip: The extra silence at the beginning is important — you'll use it later to capture a noise profile for Noise Reduction.

Step 4: Edit Your Recording

  1. Press Home then Space to play your recording from the beginning. Listen for any mistakes, long pauses, or "ums" and "ahs."
  2. Trim excess silence: Select the long silence at the beginning and end, leaving about 0.5 seconds of quiet at each edge, and press Del.
  3. Remove mistakes: Zoom in (Ctrl+1), select the mistake, preview the cut (C), and press Del if it sounds good.
  4. Check flow: Play the whole recording again. Make sure edits sound natural and there are no jarring jumps.

Step 5: Apply Noise Reduction (If Needed)

If you hear background noise (hum, hiss, fan noise):

  1. Select a short section (1–2 seconds) of pure silence where only background noise is present (no speech).
  2. Go to Effect > Noise Reduction and click Get Noise Profile.
  3. Select the entire track (Ctrl+A).
  4. Go to Effect > Noise Reduction again and click OK (default settings are usually fine for a first pass).

Step 6: Normalize Volume

  1. Select the entire track (Ctrl+A).
  2. Go to Effect > Normalize.
  3. Set "Normalize peak amplitude to" -1.0 dB.
  4. Check "Remove DC offset" if available.
  5. Click OK.

This ensures your audio is at a consistent, professional volume level.

Step 7: Export

Export your finished recording in two formats:

  1. WAV — Go to File > Export Audio, choose WAV (Microsoft), name it intro_yourname.wav, and click Export.
  2. MP3 — Go to File > Export Audio, choose MP3, set the bitrate to 192 kbps, name it intro_yourname.mp3, and click Export.

Fill in the Metadata tags when prompted:

  • Title: Personal Introduction
  • Artist: Your Name

Finally, save your Audacity project as well: File > Save Project > Save Project As.

Clear AudioVoice is easy to understand with minimal background noise.

Submission Checklist

Before You Submit, Confirm:

  • ☐ Script was written before recording.
  • ☐ Microphone was set up and levels were tested before the real recording.
  • ☐ Recording features your name, interests, and a fun fact.
  • ☐ Excess silence at the beginning and end is trimmed.
  • ☐ Mistakes and filler words are removed.
  • ☐ Noise Reduction was applied (if background noise was present).
  • ☐ Audio is normalized (peaks near -1 dB).
  • ☐ Exported as WAV file.
  • ☐ Exported as MP3 at 192 kbps.
  • ☐ Metadata tags (Title, Artist) are filled in.
  • ☐ Final length is between 45 and 75 seconds.
  • ☐ Audacity project file (.aup3) is saved.
