Toolbar & Playback Controls

Master playback, navigation, zooming, and looping with keyboard shortcuts

Playback Essentials

Efficient audio editing depends on being able to quickly play, pause, skip, and loop your audio. Learning the keyboard shortcuts in this lesson will dramatically speed up your workflow.

Core Keyboard Shortcuts

ShortcutAction
SpacePlay / Stop — Starts playback from the cursor. Press again to stop and return the cursor to where playback began.
Shift+SpaceLoop Play — Plays the selected region on repeat until you press Stop.
RRecord — Begins recording on a new track (or appends in certain modes).
PPause / Resume — Pauses playback or recording in place. Press again to continue.
HomeSkip to Start — Moves the cursor to the very beginning of the project.
EndSkip to End — Moves the cursor to the very end of the project.
Tip: Get into the habit of pressing Home then Space to play your project from the beginning. You'll do this constantly.

Play vs. Stop vs. Pause

Understanding the difference between Stop and Pause is important:

  • Stop (Space) — Ends playback and returns the cursor to where it was before you pressed Play. This way you can listen to the same section again.
  • Pause (P) — Freezes playback in place. The cursor stays where it stopped. Pressing Pause again (or Space) resumes from that point.

Use Stop when you want to re-listen to a section. Use Pause when you want to resume from where you left off.

Zoom Controls

Zooming in and out lets you see your waveform at different levels of detail. Zoom in to make precise edits; zoom out to see the big picture.

ShortcutAction
Ctrl+1Zoom In — Zooms into the waveform for more detail.
Ctrl+3Zoom Out — Zooms out to see more of the timeline.
Ctrl+2Zoom to Normal — Returns to the default zoom level.
Ctrl+EZoom to Selection — Zooms so the selected region fills the screen.
Ctrl+FFit Project in Window — Zooms out so the entire project is visible.
Ctrl+Scroll WheelZoom In/Out — Scroll up to zoom in, scroll down to zoom out.
Tip: Ctrl+F is incredibly useful when you've zoomed in too far and lost your place. It instantly shows your entire project.

Navigation

Beyond the Home and End keys, you can navigate your project in several ways:

  • Click on the Timeline (the ruler at the top) — Positions the cursor at that point in time.
  • Click on the Waveform (with the Selection tool active) — Places the cursor at that point in the audio.
  • Scroll Wheel — Scrolls the view left and right along the timeline.
  • Left/Right Arrow Keys — Nudge the cursor by a small amount. Hold Shift while pressing to extend the selection.

Looping a Section

Looping is essential when you want to listen to a section repeatedly — for example, to decide exactly where to cut.

  1. Use the Selection tool (F1) to click and drag across the region you want to loop.
  2. Press Shift+Space to begin loop playback.
  3. The selected region will play over and over until you press Space to stop.

You can also enable the Loop button in the Transport toolbar (the circular arrows icon). When the Loop button is active, pressing Space will automatically loop the selected region instead of playing through once.

Transport Menu Options

The Transport menu contains additional playback options you may find useful:

  • Play/Stop and Set Cursor — Stops playback and moves the cursor to where playback stopped (instead of snapping back).
  • Play Cut Preview (C) — Plays a short preview of what the audio would sound like if you deleted the current selection. Great for checking edits before committing.
  • Transport Options > Sound Activation Level — Starts recording only when sound exceeds a threshold (useful for hands-free recording).

Timeline Scrubbing

Scrubbing lets you drag through the timeline and hear audio in real time as you move the cursor, similar to "scrubbing" tape across a playback head. To scrub:

  1. Hover your cursor over the Timeline ruler at the top.
  2. Click and drag left or right — you'll hear the audio at the speed and direction you drag.

Scrubbing is helpful for finding a specific moment in a recording (like the exact start of a word) without having to play through the entire track.

Key Takeaways

  • Space is your most-used key — it toggles Play/Stop.
  • Shift+Space loops the selected region.
  • Ctrl+1 / Ctrl+3 zoom in and out; Ctrl+F fits the whole project on screen.
  • Home and End jump to the start and end of the project.
  • Use scrubbing on the timeline to quickly locate specific moments in your audio.
