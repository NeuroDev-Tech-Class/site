Activity: Movie Poster Design

Create a Professional-Quality Movie Poster in GIMP

Overview

In this activity, you will design a movie poster for a real or imaginary film. You'll combine many of the skills you've learned — text styling, image compositing, layer masks, filters, and blend modes — into a single polished design piece.

Requirements

Canvas Setup

  • Poster size: at least 11 × 17 inches at 150 DPI (that's 1650 × 2550 pixels).
  • Orientation: portrait (vertical) is traditional for movie posters.
  • Color mode: RGB.

Required Elements

Your finished poster must include all of the following:

  1. Title text — the movie title, prominently displayed with at least one styled effect:
    • Drop shadow, or
    • Gradient fill, or
    • Glow/outline effect, or
    • Any other creative text treatment
  2. At least one composited photo or image — a character, scene, or object placed into your poster design. Use a layer mask to blend it into the composition.
  3. A background — this can be:
    • A gradient
    • A textured surface
    • A photograph
    • A painted scene
    • Or a combination of the above
  4. Tagline text — a short, catchy phrase that hints at the movie's story (e.g., "In space, no one can hear you scream").
  5. Credit block — the small text at the bottom of the poster listing the director, starring actors, and production company. This is a standard element of all movie posters. Use a narrow, condensed font if possible.

Technical Requirements

  • Use at least 3 layers (most posters will naturally have many more).
  • Apply at least 1 filter effect (blur, drop shadow, lighting, artistic, etc.).
  • Use at least 1 layer mask for compositing or blending.

Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1: Plan Your Poster

Before opening GIMP, decide on:

  • The movie title and genre (action, comedy, horror, sci-fi, drama, etc.)
  • The mood and color palette (dark and moody? bright and colorful?)
  • What imagery you want to feature
  • A tagline

Sketch a rough layout on paper or in your head — where will the title go? Where will the main image be? Where will the credits block sit?

Step 2: Create the Canvas

Open GIMP and go to File > New. Set the size to 1650 × 2550 pixels (or larger). Set the resolution to 150 DPI.

Step 3: Build the Background

On the bottom layer, create your background. Ideas:

  • Apply a gradient (e.g., dark at the bottom fading to a brighter color at the top).
  • Place a photo and adjust with Levels and Color Balance to set the mood.
  • Apply a Gaussian Blur if using a photo background so it doesn't compete with the foreground.

Step 4: Add the Main Image

Import your main character or scene image as a new layer. Use the Scale and Move tools to position it. Add a layer mask and paint with a soft black brush to blend the image seamlessly into the background.

Step 5: Add the Title

Use the Text tool (T) to place the movie title. Choose a font that matches the genre (bold and imposing for action, elegant for drama, distressed for horror). Apply at least one effect:

  • Drop Shadow — Filters > Light and Shadow > Drop Shadow
  • Gradient Fill — Alpha to Selection, then fill with a gradient on a new layer
  • Glow — Alpha to Selection, Grow, Feather, fill with a bright color on a layer behind the text

Step 6: Add Tagline and Credits

Place the tagline below or above the title in a smaller, complementary font. At the very bottom of the poster, add the credit block in a small, condensed font. Keep the credit block at a small size — it's a design element, not meant to be the focus.

Step 7: Apply Finishing Touches

  • Adjust layer blend modes for creative effects.
  • Apply a subtle vignette (darken the edges) to draw the eye toward the center.
  • Review the layer stack and clean up any unused layers.
  • Check the overall composition — does the eye flow naturally from the image to the title to the tagline?

Step 8: Save and Export

  1. Save the project as an XCF file (Ctrl+S).
  2. Export the finished poster as a high-quality PNG or JPEG (Ctrl+Shift+E).

Activity Checklist

  • ☐ Canvas is at least 11×17 inches at 150 DPI
  • ☐ Title text with at least one styled effect (shadow, gradient, glow)
  • ☐ At least one composited photo/image
  • ☐ Background (gradient, texture, or photo)
  • ☐ Tagline text
  • ☐ Credit block at the bottom
  • ☐ At least 3 layers used
  • ☐ At least 1 filter effect applied
  • ☐ At least 1 layer mask used
  • ☐ Project saved as XCF and exported as PNG or JPEG
