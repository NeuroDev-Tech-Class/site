2D Digital Art — GIMP

Final Project: Portfolio Showcase

Demonstrate Everything You've Learned in Three Finished Pieces

Overview

For your final project, you will create a portfolio of three original pieces that demonstrate the skills and techniques you've learned throughout this course. Each piece focuses on a different area of image editing, and together they showcase your range as a digital artist.

The Three Pieces

Piece 1: Edited / Restored Photo

Take a photo (one you've taken yourself, a family photo, or a public-domain image) and apply a complete editing and retouching workflow. Your finished image should demonstrate:

  • Color correction — use Levels, Curves, and/or Color Balance to fix exposure and white balance.
  • Retouching — use the Clone Stamp and/or Healing Tool to remove blemishes, scratches, distracting elements, or other imperfections.
  • Enhancement — apply sharpening (Unsharp Mask), noise reduction, or selective adjustments to improve the final image.

The goal is a polished, professional-looking photograph that shows clear improvement over the original.

Piece 2: Original Graphic Design

Create an original graphic design piece from scratch. Choose one of the following:

  • Poster — a movie poster, event poster, concert poster, or informational poster (at least 11 × 17 inches at 150 DPI).
  • Logo — a logo and wordmark for a real or fictional brand, company, or project (with transparent background, exported at multiple sizes).
  • Social media graphic — a set of 2–3 coordinated graphics (banner, profile image, post) for a social media account.

Your design must include styled text, intentional use of color and layout, and at least one composited image element or applied filter effect.

Piece 3: Creative Composition or Digital Painting

Create a piece that showcases your creativity. Choose one of the following:

  • Photo composite — combine elements from multiple images into a seamless, creative scene using layer masks, blend modes, and color matching.
  • Digital painting — paint an original artwork from scratch using GIMP's brush tools, a layered workflow, and color theory principles.
  • Mixed-media piece — combine photo elements with painted elements, text, and effects to create a unique artistic work.

This is your chance to be creative. There are no strict requirements for subject matter — express yourself!

Technical Requirements

Each of your three pieces must demonstrate at least 3 different techniques from the skills learned in this course. Techniques include (but are not limited to):

Category Techniques
Selection Rectangle/Ellipse Select, Fuzzy Select, Select by Color, Quick Mask, Feathering
Layers Multiple layers, layer groups, blend modes, opacity adjustments
Color Correction Levels, Curves, Hue-Saturation, Color Balance, Desaturate
Retouching Clone Stamp, Healing Tool, Smudge, Blur/Sharpen
Text Text tool, styled text, text effects, text along path
Paths Bézier paths, path to selection, stroke path
Masks Layer masks, alpha channel manipulation
Filters Blur, Sharpen, Distort, Light and Shadow, Artistic filters
Painting Paintbrush, custom brushes, dynamics, digital painting workflow
Transform Scale, Rotate, Perspective, Flip, Crop

Written Descriptions

For each of your three pieces, write a short description (3–5 sentences) that explains:

  1. What the piece is and what you were aiming to achieve.
  2. Which GIMP tools and techniques you used to create it.
  3. Any challenges you faced and how you solved them.

These descriptions can be submitted as a separate document or typed directly into your project submission.

Deliverables

Submit the following:

  • 3 XCF project files — one for each piece, with all layers intact.
  • 3 exported images — one PNG or JPEG export of each finished piece.
  • Written descriptions — one for each piece (3–5 sentences each).

Evaluation Criteria

Criteria Description
Technique Variety Each piece uses at least 3 different techniques from the course. A wide range of techniques across all 3 pieces demonstrates thorough learning.
Visual Quality Pieces are well-crafted with attention to detail — clean edges, proper color, well-organized layers, appropriate resolution, and professional polish.
Creativity Pieces show original thinking, creative problem-solving, and personal expression. Work goes beyond simply following instructions.
Written Descriptions Clear, specific descriptions that accurately explain the tools and techniques used in each piece.

Activity Checklist

  • ☐ Piece 1: Edited/restored photo with color correction and retouching
  • ☐ Piece 2: Original graphic design (poster, logo, or social media graphic)
  • ☐ Piece 3: Creative composition or digital painting
  • ☐ Each piece demonstrates at least 3 different techniques
  • ☐ Written description for each piece (3–5 sentences)
  • ☐ 3 XCF project files saved with all layers
  • ☐ 3 exported PNG or JPEG images
Tip: Start early and work on one piece at a time. Don't try to rush all three at the end. Your portfolio is a showcase of your best work — give each piece the time and attention it deserves.
