## Protecting yourself online

- Don’t change passwords often - this encourages you to pick weak, easy-to-crack passwords. Instead choose a strong password the first time and use a different one for each site in case one is compromised. Keep track of your passwords (Google Chrome and iCloud Keychain).
- Use two-factor authentication.
- [https://haveibeenpwned.com/](https://haveibeenpwned.com/)
- Don’t click on suspicious links. DO NOT share personal information online. (Look at some examples of phishing.)
- Don’t open random `.exe` files if you don’t completely trust the source.
- [https://www.udemy.com/course/computer-literacy/learn/lecture/15741298#overview](https://www.udemy.com/course/computer-literacy/learn/lecture/15741298#overview)

## Do I need a VPN?

Maybe (usually not.)

Nowadays it’s harder for strangers to snoop and see data; over 90% of web connections are encrypted. Very little need to waste time and money on a VPN.

If you’re not being stalked, living in a country where Internet is censored, or trying to watch Netflix shows that aren’t available in your area, you’re probably fine.

Instead use ad blockers like uBlock Origin and privacy extensions like Privacy Badger to protect yourself from advertisers.

## Why is my computer slow?

- Run Storage Sense to remove unnecessary files
- Defragment and Optimize Drive
- Uninstall unused programs
- Stop programs from running on startup
- Scan for viruses
- Check for updates
- Switch to “High Performance” settings
- Check disk drive
- If all else fails: reformat & reinstall
