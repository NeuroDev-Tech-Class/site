In this exercise you will look at real hardware: first the outside of your own laptop, then the inside of a desktop computer. Your coach will be with you when you open the desktop, so you can ask questions as you go.

Part 1: Your laptop

  1. Find every port on your laptop. For each one, say what it is for and which cable plugs into it. The Cables and Ports reading has pictures if you get stuck.
  2. Using only your laptop and anything plugged into it, find at least two input devices (they send information into the computer) and two output devices (they send information out of it).

Part 2: Inside a desktop

Use one of the tech class desktops, with your coach.

  1. Make sure the computer is completely shut down, then unplug every cable.
  2. Before you touch anything inside, touch a metal part of the case to discharge static electricity, which can damage components. Keep screws in a cup so none get lost.
  3. Open the case. Your coach will show you where the screws or latches are.
  4. Find each of these parts. Write down what you can see on each one: brand, size, labels, or numbers.
    • CPU (it sits under the cooler; you may only see the cooler)
    • Motherboard
    • RAM
    • Storage drive
    • Power supply
    • Graphics card, if there is one
  5. Find the ports on the back of the desktop and match each one to its cable.
  6. Close the case, plug everything back in and turn the computer on to check it still works.

Tip: The Hardware and Software reading has pictures of each part if you want to check what you found.
