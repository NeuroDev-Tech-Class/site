  3. Paste the text into your document without its formatting.
  4. Cut a sentence from one part of your document and paste it somewhere else.
  5. Use a shortcut to find the word "id" and replace it with something else.
  6. Delete a sentence. Use a shortcut to undo that, and then redo it.
  7. Make a sentence bold, underlined and italic.
  8. Use Home and End to jump to the start and end of a line, then use shortcuts to jump to the start and end of the whole document.
  9. Select all of the text at once and delete it.
  10. Use a shortcut to open File Explorer, then select several files or folders with Ctrl + click.
  11. Use a shortcut to open a new File Explorer window, then another to create a new folder.
  12. Use a shortcut to take a screenshot of your whole screen, then use the Snipping Tool shortcut to capture a smaller area.
  13. Open your Screenshots folder. Cut the two images you just made and paste them onto your desktop.
  14. Use a shortcut to switch back to File Explorer, and another to close it.
