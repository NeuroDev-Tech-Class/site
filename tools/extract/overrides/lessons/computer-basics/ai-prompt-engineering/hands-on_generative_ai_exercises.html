Now that you have a ChatGPT or Gemini account, try these three exercises. They'll help you write better prompts, and they'll show you some of the limits of generative AI. Keep notes as you go: you'll write about each exercise in the form at the bottom of this page.

Exercise 1: Observe the Randomness

Ask the AI the same question 3 separate times, in separate chats. Notice how the answers change and how they stay the same. Then try asking the question in a different way. The more complex your prompt, the more interesting the results.

This randomness is part of what makes generative AI so powerful. Without it, the answers would be generic and much less helpful.

Exercise 2: Find the Limits

Try to get the AI to tell you something untrue. A good way is to ask about a niche topic you know a lot about, such as a side character in a lesser-known book you've read.

Sometimes the AI will "hallucinate": it confidently tells you something completely made up. Keep asking harder questions and note any mistakes or inventions.

It can be wrong about any subject; it's just easier to notice on a subject you know well. Always fact-check!

Exercise 3: Synthesize a Novel Idea

Ask the AI to come up with something nobody has imagined before. For example, pick 3 random ingredients and ask for a recipe, or ask it to write a new episode of one of your favorite shows.

This is what sets AI chatbots apart from search engines like Google. They aren't just finding existing information; they create something new by building on patterns they've learned.
